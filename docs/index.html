Fantasy Live Score

All your fantasy football teams in one place — on the iPhone Lock Screen, across platforms.

Plenty of people play fantasy football in more than one league, on more than one platform. Today that means one app per platform, and no single place to see how the weekend is actually going.

Fantasy Live Score is a read-only companion app that puts every team you manage side by side: live scores, projections, win probability, and your starting lineup — in the app, in Home Screen widgets, and in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

What it does

Every league, one app

Swipe between leagues. Each one keeps its own score, its own widget and its own Lock Screen activity.

Live scoring and win probability

Scores update through the week, with a projected final score and the odds of winning your matchup.

Scoring alerts

A notification when one of your starters scores — bundled and rate-limited, so a Sunday does not turn into twenty buzzes.

Any week of the season

Look back at a finished week or ahead at who you play next.
